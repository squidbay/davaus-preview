How it works

Your planter, now a precision cover-crop seeder.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Choose the plate for your seed

    Small-cell discs for radish, turnip, crimson clover and similar seeds. The rate chart for your meter and seed gives the plate and settings.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Swap into your meter

    Fits John Deere vacuum meters (not ExactEmerge), Kinze brush meters (with backer plate) and Precision Planting.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Plant with planter precision

    The singulation, depth and spacing advantages of a planter — on cover crops and small grains.
