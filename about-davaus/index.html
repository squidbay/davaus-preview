About DAVAUS

A small engineering and fabrication company with farm roots.

Founded in northeast Indiana, DAVAUS designs and builds retrofit products for equipment the founders run on their own family farms.

How we work

Science, technology, nature, practicality.

Problems are found in the field, prototyped and fabricated in-house, and tested across seasons on the home farms.
