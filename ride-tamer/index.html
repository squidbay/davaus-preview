How it works

Suspension where the mower has none.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Bolts to your front fork

    A retrofit assembly for the existing front fork. 45-minute install.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Trailing arm + elastomer

    More than 5 in. of vertical travel with even compression and rebound.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Smoother ride, protected frame

    Less bucking and bouncing for the operator, less stress on the mower frame and a more even cut.
