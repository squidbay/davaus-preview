How it works

Cushion. Capture. Collect.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Cushion

    The dampener rail softens the ear’s strike on the deck plate, so fewer kernels shell off.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Capture

    Loose kernels are held between the rail and the gathering chain instead of falling to the ground.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Collect

    FingerBrushes on each chain lug sweep captured kernels into the head.
